Using CueBunny

CueBunny helps you prepare scripts, read with a teleprompter and create recordings. You keep ownership of your content and are responsible for having permission to record, use and share it. Check exported media and captions before publishing. Speech recognition may make mistakes; feature availability depends on the device, language, system version and other apps involved.

Trying the app and Plus

After the introduction, you may choose a membership plan or skip the offer to explore the app. Skipping does not purchase or activate Plus. The sample experience and access to previously saved content follow the access rules shown in the app. Plus access requires a valid Apple-verified monthly subscription, yearly subscription or lifetime purchase. The actual available products, price, currency, billing period and any eligible offer are shown before purchase.

Subscriptions and introductory offers

Apple handles payment. Auto-renewable subscriptions continue at the displayed renewal price unless canceled. Any free trial converts to a paid subscription unless canceled in time. To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period or trial ends. You can manage or cancel through your Apple Account subscriptions. Apple’s cancellation instructions explain the steps.

Introductory-offer eligibility is determined by Apple. Offers within the same subscription group cannot be treated as separate repeat trials. Only the offer displayed for your account at purchase applies.

Lifetime access and restoration

Lifetime is a one-time purchase for CueBunny Plus access, with no recurring renewal. It does not automatically cancel an existing subscription; manage that subscription with Apple to avoid another charge. You can use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for purchase. Refunds and revocations may remove access. Refund requests are handled by Apple’s Report a Problem service.

Keep a copy of your work

CueBunny processes your work locally and does not provide cloud backup. Its library is excluded from device and iCloud backups. Keep important exports outside the app. Deleting the app or permanently deleting local work can remove content that the developer cannot recover. See the Privacy Policy for storage, permissions and data practices.
