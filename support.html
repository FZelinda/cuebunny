CUEBUNNY SUPPORT

A little help, when you need it.

From your first rehearsal to saving your next recording.

Contact the maintainer

Tell us your device model, iOS version, CueBunny version, the mode you used and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot with private information removed can help. You do not need to upload your script or original recording.

Contact support on GitHub

GitHub Issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not post private scripts, recordings or payment information.

For billing, cancellation or refunds, Apple’s account and purchase services can also help.

Common questions

Can I skip the membership screen?

Yes. Finish the three-page introduction, then choose a plan or skip to explore. Skipping does not start a purchase. You can try the built-in sample; Plus features show their access requirements in the app.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in CueBunny with the Apple Account used for purchase. To cancel an active subscription, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. A lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing subscription. Read Apple’s instructions.

Why is voice follow unavailable?

Voice follow and transcription require a supported device, system and language. Grant the requested microphone or speech permission and prepare the on-device model if offered. Initial model preparation may need a network connection. There is no cloud fallback. You can use constant-speed reading when local speech is unavailable.

Can I use the floating prompt with another app?

Floating Teleprompter uses iOS Picture in Picture and constant-speed scrolling. Availability and microphone access depend on iOS and the other app. Voice follow is not provided in floating mode. Test your chosen app combination before an important recording.

Why is a screen recording missing picture or sound?

ReplayKit and the app being recorded control what can be captured. Protected content, app restrictions or another recording session may prevent capture. Turn on the microphone in the system broadcast panel when you need commentary, stop using the system indicator and return to CueBunny to check the result. Cross-app broadcasting is shown only when available.

Which scripts can I import?

Import plain text, UTF-8/UTF-16 text, RTF or a text-based PDF from Files. Scanned-image PDFs do not provide text for this importer. The script limit is 100,000 characters; imported files must be no larger than 10 MB. You can also paste or write a script.

Where is my work stored?

Your library is on this device and is excluded from device and iCloud backup. Keep important exports elsewhere. Deleted scripts can be restored from Recently Deleted until permanently removed. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies saved outside the app remain at their destinations.
