What this policy covers

CueBunny is a teleprompter and recording app for iPhone and iPad. This policy covers the app and this developer website. The product is maintained through the GitHub account FZelinda. Use our support page to contact the maintainer about privacy or product questions.

CueBunny does not require an account, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or operate a server for uploading your scripts, recordings or transcriptions. We do not sell your personal information or use your content to train AI models.

Scripts, media and permissions

Your scripts, imported documents, recordings, editing settings, captions and generated exports are processed and stored on your device. File access is limited to app-owned files and files you explicitly select. Choosing a file stored with a cloud provider may cause that provider to download it to your device.

Camera permission is used for Camera Teleprompter recording. Microphone permission is used for recording video, audio or screen commentary and for voice-follow teleprompting. Speech-recognition permission is requested when required by the system’s on-device speech API. Saving an exported video to Photos requests add-only permission; CueBunny does not request access to browse your entire photo library. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

On-device speech processing

Voice follow and transcription use Apple’s on-device speech capabilities where supported by your device, system version and selected language. The older recognition path explicitly requires on-device recognition; the newer path uses the system’s local speech models. If local recognition is unavailable, CueBunny reports that limitation rather than sending audio to cloud transcription.

A required language model may need to be downloaded through Apple’s system service after you choose to prepare it. That download needs a network connection and is governed by Apple’s service practices. It does not change CueBunny’s local-only audio recognition requirement. Interface language availability does not mean speech recognition is available in every language. Recognition text used for following is processed locally; captions you save become part of your local project.

Screen recording and floating prompts

Floating Teleprompter uses iOS Picture in Picture. Screen commentary uses Apple ReplayKit. When a system broadcast is available and you explicitly start it, the recording extension writes screen and audio samples to a local shared App Group folder so CueBunny can import the result. It does not stream them to a developer server.

The shared folder may temporarily contain recording fragments, a recording-status file and a short-lived, locally issued access permit derived from your verified purchase entitlement. These support local recording and recovery. Screen recordings can include whatever the system allows to appear on screen; stop using the system recording indicator. Other apps and protected content may restrict capture.

Storage, backup and deletion

The app library and its recording folders are excluded from device and iCloud backup. CueBunny does not provide cloud sync or cloud recovery. Keep copies of important scripts and exports outside the app before removing it or changing devices. The internal library recovery snapshot is local recovery data, not an external backup.

Deleting a script moves it and its associated takes to Recently Deleted. You can restore it there or confirm permanent deletion. Permanent deletion removes that script and files no longer referenced by another retained script, including its record in the local recovery snapshot. Other media and exported copies can have independent lifetimes. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain it. Files already saved to Photos, Files or another service remain under your control at that destination.

Preferences and local activity counts

CueBunny stores introduction progress, reading settings, reading positions and camera preferences in local device preferences (UserDefaults). This avoids repeating the introduction and restores your chosen setup. The app does not transmit these preferences to us. System handling of preferences is separate from the excluded media library.

The app also keeps daily local counts of sample use, mode openings and membership actions, such as showing an offer or starting a purchase. These records contain the day, action, optional mode or plan and a count, with up to 1,000 records retained. They contain no script text, audio, payment details or personal identifier and are not uploaded. Purchase-flow diagnostics are limited to 128 in-memory events and disappear when that store or process is recreated.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes subscriptions, eligible introductory offers, lifetime purchases and payment details. CueBunny uses Apple-verified product, expiry and revocation information to determine access on your device. We do not receive your card details or operate a receipt-upload server. Apple handles its records under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

When you save or share a file, the destination you choose receives that copy and applies its own privacy practices. Exporting or sharing does not send a copy to the CueBunny maintainer.

This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, embedded third-party media or cookies. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect its services under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, your GitHub profile and message are public. The maintainer uses information you voluntarily provide to respond and troubleshoot. Do not post private scripts, recordings, payment information or other sensitive content. Support discussions may remain available until removed by you, a maintainer or GitHub as the platform permits; closing an issue does not delete it.

Your choices and contact

You can manage app permissions in iOS Settings, remove local content as described above, manage purchases through Apple, and choose what to share. We have no developer-held account or media database to search or delete. For information you voluntarily posted in support, you can request correction or removal through our support page; GitHub’s controls also apply. Applicable data-protection rights are unaffected.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children. We will update this policy and its date when our practices change.

Contact support on GitHub
